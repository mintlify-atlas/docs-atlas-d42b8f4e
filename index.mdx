PrestamoEquipo is the equipment loan management platform for the Biomedical Engineering laboratory. Students can browse available equipment, submit loan requests, and track their active loans — while administrators manage inventory, approve requests, and monitor lab activity from a central dashboard.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an account">
    Register with your name, email, career, and phone number. New accounts start as **Visitors** with read-only access to the catalog.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Request access">
    Submit an access upgrade request with your identification document. Once approved by an administrator, your account is upgraded to **Student** level, allowing you to make loan requests.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Browse and request equipment">
    Browse the equipment catalog, filter by availability, and submit loan requests specifying your start date, return date, professor, and subject.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track your loans">
    View all your pending, active, and completed loan requests from your user profile. Return equipment when you're done, or edit pending requests as needed.
  </Step>
</Steps>
