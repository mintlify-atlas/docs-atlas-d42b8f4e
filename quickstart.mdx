This guide walks you through every step from creating an account to submitting your first equipment loan request.

<Info>
  You need a Estudiante (Student) role to submit loan requests. New accounts start as Visitante (Visitor) and must request an upgrade. Steps 3 and 4 below cover this process.
</Info>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the login page">
    Open your browser and navigate to `/login`.

    If you already have an account, enter your email and password to sign in and skip to step 5. Otherwise, continue to step 2 to register.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Register a new account">
    From the login page, follow the link to `/register` and fill in the registration form.

    | Field | Description |
    |---|---|
    | `nombre` | Your first name |
    | `apellido` | Your last name |
    | `carrera` | Your degree program |
    | `telefono` | Your phone number |
    | `rol` | Your role (leave as the default) |
    | `email` | Your institutional email address |
    | `password` | Your chosen password |
    | `confirm_password` | Repeat the password to confirm |

    **Password requirements:**

    - At least 8 characters
    - At least one uppercase letter
    - At least one lowercase letter
    - At least one number

    <Warning>
      New accounts are assigned the **Visitante** role automatically. You will not be able to submit loan requests until a lab administrator upgrades your account to **Estudiante**.
    </Warning>

    After submitting, you are redirected to the login page. Sign in with your new credentials.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Request a student access upgrade">
    Once signed in as a Visitante, navigate to `/solicitarAccesoMenu`.

    Fill in the access request form:

    - **Motivo (Reason)** — Explain why you need access. Include your course, semester, and intended use of the lab equipment.
    - **Documento de identidad (ID document)** — Upload a photo or scan of your student ID or government-issued ID.

    Submit the form. Your request enters a queue for administrator review.

    <Note>
      You will remain a Visitante until an administrator approves your request. Contact your lab coordinator if approval takes longer than expected.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Wait for administrator approval">
    A lab administrator reviews your access request and either approves or rejects it.

    - **Approved** — Your role is upgraded to Estudiante. You can now submit loan requests.
    - **Rejected** — You may resubmit with additional information at `/solicitarAccesoMenu`.

    Once approved, sign out and sign back in to ensure your session reflects the updated role.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Browse the equipment catalog">
    Navigate to `/Catalogo` to view all available lab equipment.

    Use the status filter to narrow results:

    | Status | Meaning |
    |---|---|
    | `Disponible` | Equipment is ready to loan |
    | `En uso` | Currently checked out by another user |

    <Tip>
      Filter by `Disponible` before adding items to your cart to avoid requesting equipment that cannot be approved immediately.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add equipment to your cart or request directly">
    From the catalog, you have two ways to request equipment:

    **Option A — Cart**

    Add one or more items to your cart, then submit a single combined loan request for all selected items.

    **Option B — Direct request**

    Click on any equipment item to go to its detail page, then navigate to `/solicitar/<IDequipo>` (where `<IDequipo>` is the equipment's ID number).

    Fill in the loan request form:

    | Field | Description |
    |---|---|
    | `motivo` | Reason for the loan (e.g., lab practice, research project) |
    | `fecha_inicio` | Start date of the loan |
    | `fecha_entrega` | Expected return date |
    | `profesor` | Name of the supervising professor |
    | `materia` | Course or subject name |

    <Warning>
      Make sure `fecha_entrega` is after `fecha_inicio`. Requests with invalid date ranges will not be accepted.
    </Warning>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Submit and track your request">
    Review the form details and submit your loan request.

    After submission, go to `/perfilUsuario` to track the status of all your loan requests.

    | Status | Meaning |
    |---|---|
    | `pendiente` | Awaiting administrator review |
    | `activo` | Loan approved — pick up the equipment from the lab |
    | `eliminado` | Request was rejected or removed by an admin |
    | `Concluido` | Equipment returned and the loan is closed |

    <Tip>
      Include a clear, specific reason in the `motivo` field. Requests with vague descriptions are more likely to be delayed or rejected.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What's next?

Once your loan is approved, coordinate with lab staff to pick up the equipment. Return it by the `fecha_entrega` date you specified in your request.

You can view your full loan history — including active and past loans — at any time from `/perfilUsuario`.
