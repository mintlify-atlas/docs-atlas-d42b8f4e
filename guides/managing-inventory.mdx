title: 'Managing inventory'
description: 'Add, edit, and delete laboratory equipment records, manage equipment status, and upload equipment images.'
---

<Note>
  Inventory management requires the **Administrator** permission.
</Note>

The inventory section at `/Inventario` is where you manage all equipment records in the laboratory system.

## Browsing inventory

Navigate to `/Inventario` to see the full equipment list. Use the `filtro` query parameter (or the filter controls in the UI) to narrow the list:

| Filter value | What it shows |
|---|---|
| `todos` | All equipment regardless of status |
| `Disponible` | Equipment available for new loans |
| `En uso` | Equipment currently on loan or pending approval |

## Equipment status

Each piece of equipment has one of two status values:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `Disponible` | Available — can be assigned to a new loan request |
| `En uso` | Currently on loan — cannot be assigned until the loan is closed |

Status transitions are managed automatically when you approve or close loan requests. You can also set status manually when editing an equipment record.

## Adding new equipment

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the add equipment form">
    Navigate to `/AgregarEquipo`. The form collects the basic fields needed to register a new item.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the required fields">
    Complete the form fields listed below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload an image (optional)">
    Use the image upload control to attach a photo of the equipment. See [Image uploads](#image-uploads) for details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit the form">
    Submit to `/AgregarEquipoAplica`. The record is inserted into the `equiposLab` table and immediately appears in the inventory list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Fields on the add equipment form

<ParamField path="body.nombre" type="string" required>
  The display name of the equipment item.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="body.descripcion" type="string">
  A general description of the equipment and its purpose.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="body.estado" type="string" required>
  Initial status of the equipment. Typically set to `Disponible` for new items.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="body.fechaAdquisicion" type="string" required>
  The date the equipment was acquired. Use the date picker in the form.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="body.ubicacion" type="string">
  Physical location of the equipment within the laboratory.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="body.imagen" type="string">
  Path or filename of the uploaded equipment image.
</ParamField>

## Editing equipment

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the equipment record">
    Go to `/Inventario` and locate the item you want to update.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the edit form">
    Navigate to `/EditarS/<IDequipo>`, replacing `<IDequipo>` with the equipment's ID. The form pre-populates all current values.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update the fields">
    Modify any of the fields listed below. All fields from the add form are available here, plus the extended technical fields.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save changes">
    Submit to `/Editar/<IDequipo>`. The record in `equiposLab` is updated immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<ParamField path="body.nombre" type="string" required>

## Deleting equipment

<Warning>
  Deleting equipment is irreversible. The system performs a **cascade delete**: all observations and loan requests linked to the equipment are permanently removed before the equipment record itself is deleted.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Locate the equipment record">
    Find the item in `/Inventario`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Initiate deletion">
    Navigate to `/EliminarEquipo/<IDequipo>`. No confirmation form is shown — the deletion executes immediately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify removal">
    Return to `/Inventario` to confirm the equipment no longer appears in the list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Image uploads

Both the add and edit forms include an image upload control. When you upload an image:

- The file is saved to `static/uploads/equipos/` and the path is stored in the `Imagen` column of `equiposLab`.
- Accepted formats: `.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.png`. Other formats are rejected with an error message.
- Maximum file size: **2 MB**.
- Images are automatically resized to fit within 800×800 pixels and re-saved as JPEG with quality 85.
- To replace an existing image, open the edit form and upload a new file. The previous path reference is overwritten.
