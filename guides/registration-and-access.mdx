## Create an account

New accounts are created at `/register`. Fill in all required fields and submit the form to get started.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the registration page">
    Navigate to `/register` in your browser.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in your personal information">
    Complete all required fields:

    | Field              | Description                              |
    | ------------------ | ---------------------------------------- |
    | `nombre`           | Your first name                          |
    | `apellido`         | Your last name                           |
    | `carrera`          | Your academic program or career          |
    | `telefono`         | Your contact phone number                |
    | `rol`              | Your role at the institution             |
    | `email`            | Your institutional email address         |
    | `password`         | Your chosen password (see rules below)   |
    | `confirm_password` | Repeat your password to confirm          |
  </Step>
  <Step title="Meet the password requirements">
    Your password must satisfy all of the following rules:

    - At least **8 characters** long
    - Contains at least one **uppercase** letter
    - Contains at least one **lowercase** letter
    - Contains at least one **digit**

    <Warning>
      Passwords that do not meet all four requirements will be rejected. You will need to correct the password before the form can be submitted.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit the form">
    Once all fields are valid, submit the form. Your account is created with `Permiso = 'Visitante'` automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Permission levels

PrestamoEquipo uses three permission levels to control what each user can do.

| Level          | Description                                                            |
| -------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `Visitante`    | Can browse the catalog but cannot request or borrow equipment          |
| `Estudiante`   | Can request equipment, manage loans, and use the cart                  |
| `Administrador`| Full access, including approving requests and managing the system      |

<Note>
  All new accounts start as `Visitante`. You must request an upgrade to `Estudiante` before you can borrow equipment.
</Note>

## Request a permission upgrade

To gain `Estudiante` access and start requesting equipment, you must submit an access request for an administrator to review.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the access request page">
    Navigate to `/solicitarAccesoMenu`. This page is available to all logged-in users regardless of their current permission level.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload your ID document">
    Attach a photo of your student ID or other valid identification. The field accepts common image formats.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write a reason for the request">
    In the `motivo` field, briefly explain why you need equipment access — for example, the course you are enrolled in or the project you are working on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit the request">
    Send the form to `POST /SolicitarAcceso`. Your request enters a queue for administrator review.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Write a clear and specific reason in the `motivo` field. Requests with vague explanations may take longer to process or be declined.
</Tip>

Once an administrator approves your request, your account permission is updated to `Estudiante` and you gain full access to request and borrow equipment.

## Log in

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the login page">
    Navigate to `/login`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your credentials">
    Provide the `email` and `password` you registered with, then submit the form.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  After several failed login attempts your session may be restricted. Make sure you are using the email address you registered with.
</Warning>

## Log out

To end your session, navigate to `/logout`. Your session data is cleared immediately and you are redirected to the login page.

<Tip>
  Always log out when using a shared or public computer to prevent unauthorized access to your account.
</Tip>
