## Overview

Individual loan requests let you borrow one piece of equipment at a time. The request goes to an administrator for approval before the loan is active.

<Note>
  You must have `Estudiante` or `Administrador` permission to submit requests. If your account is still `Visitante`, see [Registration and access](/guides/registration-and-access) to request an upgrade.
</Note>

## Submit a loan request

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find an available item">
    Open the catalog at `/Catalogo` and locate the item you want. Only equipment with `Disponible` status can be requested. Items marked `En uso` are unavailable until returned by the current borrower.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the request form">
    Click **Request** on the equipment card. This navigates to `/solicitar/<IDequipo>`, where `<IDequipo>` is the unique identifier of that item.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the loan request form">
    Complete all fields before submitting:

    <ParamField body="motivo" type="string" required>
      The reason you need to borrow this equipment. Be specific — administrators use this to evaluate your request.
    </ParamField>

    <ParamField body="fecha_inicio" type="string" required>
      The date you need the equipment from. Use the date picker or enter the date in the required format.
    </ParamField>

    <ParamField body="fecha_entrega" type="string" required>
      The date by which you will return the equipment.
    </ParamField>

    <ParamField body="profesor" type="string" required>
      The name of the professor supervising the activity for which you need the equipment.
    </ParamField>

    <ParamField body="materia" type="string" required>
      The subject or course the loan is associated with.
    </ParamField>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit the request">
    Click **Send request**. The form posts to `POST /enviar_solicitud` along with the `equipo_id` of the selected item.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for approval">
    Your request is now in **Pendiente** (pending) state. An administrator will review it and either approve or reject it. You can track the status from your profile at `/perfilUsuario`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Availability requirement

<Warning>
  If the equipment you selected becomes `En uso` between when you opened the form and when you submitted it, the request will be rejected. Return to the catalog and check availability before trying again.
</Warning>

## After submission

Once submitted, your request appears in the **Solicitudes pendientes** section of your profile. From there you can:

- **Edit** the request details before an administrator acts on it
- **Cancel** the request if you no longer need the item

See [Managing loans](/guides/managing-loans) for details on both actions.
